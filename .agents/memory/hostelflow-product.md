---
name: HostelFlow product scope
description: Product positioning and the security invariant for the hostel movement prototype.
---

HostelFlow is a college hostel movement and security platform, not a traditional ERP. It connects student requests, role-based approvals, gate checks, parent/staff updates, and live operations.

**Why:** The product brief makes security the priority: a QR identifies an authorized movement, while identity verification and explicit gate confirmation are separate steps.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinct QR, biometric, and gate-confirmation steps across exit and return flows. Keep parent views limited to their child and role views scoped to their responsibilities.
